Privacy Policy

License Plate Hunt. Last updated: November 8, 2026

License Plate Hunt is a road trip game. It has an iPhone app and a web version. This policy explains what each one does with data.

Your game data

Your trips, spotted plates, reward settings and language are saved only on your device. There are no accounts, and we don't receive or store this data.

In the web version, a backup file you export stays wherever you choose to save it.

Ads in the iPhone app

The iPhone app shows banner ads from Google AdMob. The web version has no ads, and it loads nothing from other companies.

We set every ad request in the app to:

  • ask for non-personalized ads, for all users
  • use Google's child age treatment, for every user
  • allow only ads rated suitable for general audiences

The app doesn't show Apple's tracking permission prompt and doesn't share its game data with Google or give it your precise location.

Data Google's ad software collects

Google declares that its ad software for iPhone apps can collect the data below. Google's child age treatment and non-personalized requests limit how some of it is used.

  • Device ID. Used for advertising and analytics. Google declares it as linked to you and as usable for tracking.
  • Advertising data, such as which ads you've seen. Used for advertising and analytics, and linked to you.
  • Product interaction, such as taps on ads. Used for advertising and analytics, and linked to you.
  • Coarse location, a general area rather than your exact position. Used for advertising and analytics, and linked to you.
  • Performance and other diagnostic data. Used for advertising and analytics, and not linked to you.
  • Crash data. Used for analytics, and not linked to you.

Google handles this data under its own policies. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's privacy policy.

Your consent choices

Where the law requires it, including in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your choice through Google's consent form before it starts its ad software. You can change that choice at any time in Settings → Privacy Options.

In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form lists the standard purposes ad partners may request, such as personalized advertising and precise geolocation. Whatever you choose there, this app only requests non-personalized ads and does not share precise location.

Children

This is a general-audience game. We don't ask for anyone's age, name or contact details, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you think we hold information about a child, contact us and we'll help.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

License Plate Hunt

License Plate Hunt is a road trip game. Spot license plates from the US, Canada, Mexico and China, and each plate you find earns a reward. Your trips are saved on your device, and your earnings are saved when you end a trip. It has an iPhone app and a web version, in English and Chinese.

Questions

How do rewards work?

Every plate has a reward amount, set by the reward template you choose. When you spot a plate, its amount is added to the trip. When you end the trip, the total is saved to History. You can show rewards as money, points, stars or a unit of your own in Settings → Reward setup.

What's the difference between Proximity to Home and Flat Rate?

Proximity to Home pays more the farther a plate traveled from your home state. With the default amounts:

  • Within 400 mi of home: $0.50
  • 400–900 mi away: $1.00
  • 900–1,600 mi away: $2.00
  • 1,600–2,400 mi away: $3.50
  • Over 2,400 mi away: $5.00

Flat Rate pays the same for every plate, $2.00 by default.

To switch, open Settings → Reward setup and tap a template. To change its amounts, tap Edit next to it. Changing your home state in Reward setup updates Proximity to Home's amounts.

How do I back up and restore the web version?

The web version saves your game in your browser. Clearing your browser data, deleting the app from your home screen or changing phones removes it, so make a backup before a trip.

  • Back up: Settings → Backup → Export data. Save the file somewhere safe, such as Files or your email.
  • Restore: Settings → Backup → Import data, then choose the backup file. On a new install, you can also choose Restore from backup during setup. Restoring replaces all trips, templates and settings on that device.

The iPhone app keeps your game on your iPhone and is included in your iPhone's iCloud or computer backups.

How do I report an ad?

The iPhone app shows a small banner ad on the Home screen. If you see an ad that seems inappropriate, open Settings → Report an ad. This starts an email to us with your app version filled in. Please describe the ad, and include a screenshot if you can. If Mail isn't set up, the app copies our email address so you can write from any email app.

The web version has no ads.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Where the consent rules apply, such as in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, open Settings → Privacy Options in the iPhone app. See the privacy policy for details.
